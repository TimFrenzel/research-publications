## Abstract

The adoption of Machine Learning Operations (MLOps) in practice is still in its infancy and there are few common guidelines on how to effectively integrate it into existing software development practices. This paper conducts a systematic literature review and a grey literature review to derive a framework that identifies the activities involved in the adoption of MLOps and the stages in which companies evolve as they become more mature and advanced. The framework is validated in three case companies and shows how they have managed to adopt and integrate MLOps in their large-scale software development companies.

## Key Contributions

### 1. MLOps Framework
The paper proposes a comprehensive framework that identifies:
- Core activities in MLOps adoption
- Dependencies between activities
- Integration points with existing DevOps practices
- Key stakeholders and their roles

### 2. Maturity Model
A stage-based maturity model showing company evolution:
- **Stage 0**: Initial/No MLOps
- **Stage 1**: Basic MLOps practices
- **Stage 2**: Systematic MLOps integration
- **Stage 3**: Advanced MLOps with automation
- **Stage 4**: Full MLOps maturity

### 3. Adoption Guidelines
Practical guidance on:
- Starting MLOps implementation
- Overcoming common challenges
- Scaling MLOps across organization
- Measuring progress and maturity

## Key Findings

### MLOps Adoption Challenges

1. **Cultural Challenges**
   - Bridging gap between data science and operations teams
   - Changing mindset from experimentation to production
   - Building cross-functional collaboration

2. **Technical Challenges**
   - Integrating ML pipelines with existing CI/CD
   - Managing model versioning and reproducibility
   - Implementing effective monitoring

3. **Organizational Challenges**
   - Defining clear roles and responsibilities
   - Allocating resources for MLOps
   - Establishing governance frameworks

### Success Factors

1. **Executive Support**: Strong leadership commitment
2. **Cross-Functional Teams**: Collaboration between data science, engineering, and operations
3. **Incremental Approach**: Start small and scale gradually
4. **Automation Focus**: Automate repetitive tasks early
5. **Continuous Learning**: Foster culture of experimentation and learning

## Limitations

1. **Limited Case Studies**: Only three companies validated the framework
2. **General Framework**: Not industry-specific
3. **Snapshot in Time**: MLOps practices evolving rapidly
4. **Implementation Details**: High-level framework without detailed technical specifications
